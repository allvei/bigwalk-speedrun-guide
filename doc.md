## Introduction

This guide serves to be a compendium and archive of all strategies and glitches discovered for Big Walk Speedrunning.

To discuss anything about Big Walk speedrunning, you can join the discord here and find our Speedrun.com leaderboards here.

### Rationale for this Guide

At this point in time, it is very ambiguous and undefined as to how to best categorise glitches. We created this document to better organise our thoughts and create a discrete judging system on which we can base our rulings.

## Category Splits

![image](https://hackmd.io/_uploads/rkyI-83_Ml.png)

*The Big Walk Speedrun.com page as of the creation of this segment of the guide*

Right now, we have two different endings, and therefore two different categories. These are:

- **Big Goodbye** (Reach the white ending door at the northernmost part of the island)
- **Big Game** (Complete the broken orb section within the spawn area)

These are named after the relevant achievements that you get after completing these endings in game. It is likely that we will need to categorise runs based on the **most restrictive category that these runs can fit into.**

We subcategorise these into several parameters. Right now, these are:

- **Players** (how many players did you complete this run with)
    - There likely needs to be a further ruling on which category we need runs to go in. Suggestions at this time could be the **maximum amount of players** in the submitted run at any given time
- **Game Mode** (when you create or launch a save file, which option did you set)
    - These are currently 2P, 3P and 4P.
    - There likely needs to be a further ruling on having runs **only use one game mode** throughout the course of the run if save and quits are allowed mid run.
- **Challenge Skipping** (in the “accessibility” tab when creating or launching save files)
    - This option creates small control boards in front of many puzzles that allows the player to entirely skip them.
    - Again, we will likely need to specify that both Game Mode and Challenge Skipping must stay the same from when the save file was created.

Finally, the runs are split by Glitch Definitions. Currently, these are somewhat undefined and undescriptive. These are currently:

- **Unrestricted** (beat the game in any way you wish)
- **Glitched** (achieve the category ending while *also completing all of the requirements*)
    - These requirements are:
        - Open the door to the black tower
        - Place the black key in its keyhole
        - Power down both silencers
- **Glitchless** (achieve the category ending while *completing all of the requirements **and** not doing any glitches*).
    - The Speedrun.com leaderboard does not have a list of glitches that are allowed or disallowed, rather that “No glitches may be used”.
    - It is very important that glitches are given discrete and tangible definitions to further categorise runs.

### Proposals

Currently, “Glitched” is a very arbitrary way to describe “Complete all of the main *source requirements* to beat the game, and then beat the game. No other glitch restrictions are placed”. We have several suggestions on how to remedy this.

- **Rename the category to “No Major Glitches”**
    - This implies that a “Major Glitch” is skipping past the source requirement of placing the keys in all the keyholes.
- **Rename the category to “All Main Keys”**
    - This infers that the “Main Keys” are the keys found on the Beach, the Red Tower, Blue Tower, Green Tower, Yellow Tower and Black Tower.
    - This also implies that the final key found in the second green tower is not a “main key”.
- **Rename the category to “No Major Skips”**
    - This places a restriction on getting around any kind of source requirement placed by keys by skipping around them.
- **Rename the category to “No Sequence Breaks”**
    - This is a more commonly used term to indicate that breaking out of the intended sequence of the game is disallowed.

Additionally, it may be prudent to merge all of the categories as they are into just two categories: **Big Game** and **Big Goodbye**. This would make it easier to understand at a glance what the two main endings are of the game, and then within the categories, we could have different tabs for each of the glitch categories.

#### Addendum

This is less important to the glitch categorisation and more important to the general legibility and understanding of the leaderboards.

As of patch v1.4.9 and onwards, it has become increasingly clear that House House intend on patching certain clips out of the game. Therefore, downpatching to older patches where clips are easier/faster seems to be most optimal at this point in time.

A guide on how to downpatch has already been created. However, it is likely to be very useful to the community to add a **field parameter** for **which patch the run was performed on.** This can be easily achieved and will add a level of both proof and information for all runs.

## Suggestions on Categorising Glitches and Exploits

It is hard to define here the difference between a “Glitch” and an “Exploit”. For the sake of this guide, **they are the same thing**. It is more generally agreed upon that a glitch is of higher “game-breaking” potential than an exploit, and as such, it may eventually be redefined that glitches are only allowed in glitched categories, whereas exploits can be used anywhere.

### Suggestions

Many of these glitches just exist in the game and are not particularly useful to the speedrun. However, some trivialise the entire game (e.g. Item Flying) and are probably important to restrict in certain categories.

As the most restrictive category is “Glitchless” right now, it seems important that we limit which glitches are allowed and which are not, so which are exploits and which are glitches.

Here is a ***suggestion table only*** that would hopefully help to guide a major poll in future. It is clear at this point in time that a major poll is probably necessary to help guide future runs before people start to route puzzles.
