## Glitches, Exploits and General Tech List

#### Wall Clipping

Wall Clipping is defined as forcing the player through any kind of wall that you would not normally be able to walk through. This is distinct from an Item Squeeze.

**Blue House Lantern Clip:** https://youtu.be/OHknYs1KiZU?si=xNkWDc_Rk0T1rxab

This video contains several methods of completing the Blue House in the Beach area with 1P. This clip regards the second method of completing the Blue House puzzle, but note that Wall Clipping *only refers to* the method in which the player seemingly "phases" through a wall. Ledge standing is distinct from this.

#### Turnstile Headless Chicken Hat Grab

https://discord.com/channels/1535292250437976105/1535292252115566674/1543003651667402863

This glitch is newly discovered and extremely contentious. While it appears to not be "Glitching" in any way (clipping through walls, breaking physics, etc) it appears to entirely be an oversight by the devs. **This is a likely candidate to be part of the first poll, if there is one.** In my opinion, it violates the "spirit" of Glitchless, as it seems to be part of a ruleset designed to be more about completing the game "as intended".
