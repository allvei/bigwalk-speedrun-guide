### Turnstile Headless Chicken Hat Grab

- **Turnstile Headless Chicken Hat Grab:** https://discord.com/channels/1535292250437976105/1535292252115566674/1543003651667402863

This glitch is newly discovered and extremely contentious. While it appears to not be "Glitching" in any way (clipping through walls, breaking physics, etc) it appears to entirely be an oversight by the devs. **This is a likely candidate to be part of the first poll, if there is one.** In my opinion, it violates the "spirit" of Glitchless, as it seems to be part of a ruleset designed to be more about completing the game "as intended".
