## Introduction

This guide serves to be a compendium and archive of all strategies and glitches discovered for Big Walk Speedrunning.

To discuss anything about Big Walk speedrunning, you can [join the Discord here](https://discord.gg/) and find our [Speedrun.com leaderboards here](https://www.speedrun.com/).

### Rationale for this Guide

At this point in time, it is very ambiguous and undefined as to how to best categorise glitches. We created this document to better organise our thoughts and create a discrete judging system on which we can base our rulings.
